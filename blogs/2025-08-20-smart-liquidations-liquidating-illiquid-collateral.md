Smart withdrawal unlocks an entirely new class of leveraged yield strategies by making it possible to liquidate illiquid, redemption-delayed collateral assets using flash loans. We call this smart liquidation . This post will dive deep into the novel methodology of smart liquidation from a practical perspective.
